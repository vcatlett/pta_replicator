*****************
Coordinate System
*****************

Using the convention of Equation 15 of :cite:t:`Gair-2014`, we define our coordinate system by

* The origin is the solar system barycenter (SSB), but Earth is basically at :math:`\vec{x}=\vec{0}` (Eq. 67 of :cite:t:`Gair-2014`)
* :math:`\phi \in [0, 2\pi]` is the azimuthal coordinate (right ascension in radians)
* :math:`\theta \in [-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}]` is the polar coordinate (declination in radians)
* :math:`\hat{r}=\hat{k}=\mathrm{sin}\theta\mathrm{cos}\phi\hat{x}+\mathrm{sin}\theta\mathrm{sin}\phi\hat{y}+\mathrm{cos}\theta\hat{z}` is a unit vector pointing to somewhere on the sky
* :math:`\hat{\theta}=\hat{l}=\mathrm{cos}\theta\mathrm{cos}\phi\hat{x}+ \mathrm{cos}\theta\mathrm{sin}\phi\hat{y}-\mathrm{sin}\theta\hat{z}` is the polar basis vector
* :math:`\hat{\phi}=\hat{m}=-\mathrm{sin}\phi\hat{x}+\mathrm{cos}\phi\hat{y}` is the azimuthal basis vector
